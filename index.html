01 / INTRODUCTION

Learning in the age of intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to technologies that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, generating information, and supporting decision-making.

In education, AI can support personalized learning, provide feedback, assist teachers, and make educational resources more accessible.

Understanding AI in education is important because these technologies are becoming part of the way people learn and work. AI creates new opportunities, but it also requires responsible and ethical use.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

AI has a role in the classroom.

01

Personalized Learning

AI systems can help provide learning activities or explanations that respond to a student's needs and progress.

02

Learning Assistance

AI tools can help students brainstorm, explain difficult concepts, practice skills, and receive feedback when used appropriately.

03

Teacher Support

AI can assist educators with tasks such as creating learning materials, organizing information, and generating activity ideas.

Examples of AI in Education

  • AI-powered tutoring and learning assistants
  • Adaptive learning platforms
  • Automated feedback and assessment support
  • Accessibility and language-support tools
  • Educational content and activity generation

THE CLASSROOM OF TOMORROW

Technology should support learning — not replace it.

03 / IMPACT

Possibilities come with responsibility.

+

Benefits

  • Can support personalized learning
  • Can provide quick learning assistance
  • Can help educators with routine tasks
  • Can improve access to learning resources
  • Can encourage new ways of creating and learning
!

Challenges

  • AI-generated information can be inaccurate
  • Student privacy and data protection matter
  • Overreliance may weaken independent learning
  • Bias can affect AI-generated results
  • Responsible and ethical use is essential
